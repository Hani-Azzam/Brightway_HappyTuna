THE CRISIS SCENARIO

What happened:
The quality lab at HappyTuna detected salmonella on Production Line A. Some contaminated cans may already be in stores. The company must decide how to respond.

Key facts about the crisis:
- Production Line A is the older line, operational since 1995
- Salmonella in canned seafood can survive if canning temperature/pressure was insufficient
- Products from Line A may already be distributed to stores
- The company is legally required to notify the Ministry of Health within 24-48 hours
- This is HappyTuna's first major food safety incident on record

What the journalist needs to find out:
- When exactly was the contamination detected?
- Which batch numbers are affected?
- Have products already reached consumers?
- Has the company notified the regulator?
- What is the company doing to contain the situation?
- Is the CEO making public statements?
- What do independent food safety experts say about the risk?

Why this story matters:
- Public health risk — consumers may have already purchased affected products
- Company accountability — HappyTuna has a trusted family brand reputation at stake
- Regulatory response — how quickly does the Ministry of Health act?
- CEO decision quality — is leadership handling this correctly?

JOURNALIST CHARACTERISTICS (official BitriX definition)

The Journalist agent in BitriX is defined by these configurable traits:
- Investigative Depth (Low, Medium, High): how deeply the journalist digs for information
- Verification Strictness (Low, Medium, High): how rigorously facts are checked before publishing
- Sensationalism Level (Low, Medium, High): tendency to dramatize or exaggerate
- Bias (Pro-Company, Neutral, Anti-Company): editorial slant toward HappyTuna
- Independence (Low, Medium, High): reliance on official sources vs independent investigation
- Persistence (Low, Medium, High): how hard the journalist pursues a story
- Public Interest Focus (Low, Medium, High): priority given to public safety over other angles
- Source Reliance (Official Sources, Mixed Sources, Anonymous Sources): where information comes from
- Credibility (Low, Medium, High): audience trust in the journalist
- Speed of Publication (Slow, Moderate, Fast): how quickly articles are published
- Narrative Influence (Low, Medium, High): impact of articles on public perception

Our journalist profile: Neutral bias, High verification strictness, High investigative depth, Mixed sources, High credibility, Moderate publication speed, High public interest focus.
