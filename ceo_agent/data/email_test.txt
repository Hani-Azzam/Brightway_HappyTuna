Subject : URGENT - Food safety escalation, metal fragments in batch 4471 (response required within 24 hours)

Body :
Attn: Office of the CEO, HappyTuna

This office has opened a food safety escalation concerning HappyTuna canned
tuna, production batch 4471 (lot HT-4471-B).

Findings to date:
- We have received 14 independent consumer reports of hard metal fragments
  found in sealed cans from batch 4471. Three reports involve injury; one
  required medical treatment for a laceration to the mouth.
- Fragments recovered from four separate cans are consistent in composition
  and appear to originate from a failed seaming or filling component rather
  than post-purchase tampering.
- Your own customer support queue contains multiple open tickets logged
  against this batch under issue type "safety_concern". These do not appear
  to have been escalated internally.
- Batch 4471 remains available for purchase at retail. We have no record of
  a hold, withdrawal, or recall notice from HappyTuna.

This constitutes an ongoing risk of serious injury to consumers.

Required of HappyTuna within 24 hours of receipt:
1. Confirm whether you are initiating a voluntary recall of batch 4471. If
   you decline, state your reasoning in writing.
2. Issue a public consumer safety notice advising anyone holding product
   from batch 4471 to stop consuming it immediately and not to open the
   cans. Silence at this stage will be treated as a failure to warn.
3. Provide production, sanitation, and equipment maintenance records for the
   line that produced batch 4471, covering the 30 days prior to production.
4. Identify the distribution footprint of batch 4471 by region and retailer.
5. Confirm your point of contact for this matter.

Be advised that consumers are already discussing this batch publicly and
attributing injuries to your product. A prompt, accurate public statement
from HappyTuna is expected. An inaccurate one is worse than none.

Failure to respond within the stated window will result in escalation to
formal enforcement, including mandatory recall authority.

Office of Food Safety Compliance
