HAPPYTUNA COMPANY BACKGROUND — JOURNALIST REFERENCE

Company overview:
HappyTuna is one of Israel's leading canned tuna producers, headquartered in the Tel Aviv metropolitan area. Founded in 1987, the company operates a main production facility with two active production lines: Line A and Line B. Annual revenue is approximately $250 million. HappyTuna exports to 14 countries across Europe and the Middle East.

Production:
- Line A: older line, operational since 1995, handles standard tuna cans
- Line B: newer line, completed in 2024, handles premium products
- The facility runs two shifts, 6 days a week
- Quality control lab is on-site and runs daily batch testing

Key executives:
- CEO: Sarah Goldberg — has led the company since 2018
- COO: responsible for production operations and supply chain
- Head of Quality: oversees lab testing and safety compliance

Regulatory history:
- 2019: Minor labelling violation, resolved with corrective action
- 2021: Routine inspection passed with no findings
- 2023: Line B equipment upgrade flagged for delayed maintenance reporting
- No prior product recalls on record

Distribution:
HappyTuna products are distributed to major supermarket chains across Israel and exported to retail partners in Europe. Products typically reach store shelves within 3-5 days of production.

Key facts for crisis coverage:
- If contamination is found on Line A, affected batches may already be in stores
- The company has a public-facing website where it posts official statements
- Media contacts go through the press office: press@happytuna.com
- In past incidents, the company has been cooperative with regulators
- The CEO is known for being media-cautious — statements tend to be measured
